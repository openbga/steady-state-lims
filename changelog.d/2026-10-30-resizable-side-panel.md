title: Resize the side panel and widen it for correction history
pr: 34
release: 0.1.2
---
Drag the left edge of a side panel, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to change its width. The width is remembered per page on this computer, and **Show correction history** widens the panel automatically so the Reason column is visible.
