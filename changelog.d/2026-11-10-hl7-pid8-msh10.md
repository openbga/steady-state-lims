PID-8 in every HL7 message the LIS sends is a standard code: F, M, A, N, O or U from the recorded text, and empty when nothing is recorded. **Send test message** now uses a new MSH-10 each time (`TEST-` and a unique ID), and MSH-11 stays `T`. The patient record is unchanged.
