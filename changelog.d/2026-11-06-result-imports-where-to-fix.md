The panel of a quarantined row has **Where to fix** links: Worklist, Open runs or Container lookup for an unknown or ambiguous label, the run for a closed or failed run, the file template or instrument for unmapped codes, units or scale, and the existing result for a conflicting duplicate. Each open row also offers **Retry import** in its menu and panel.
