The Analyzer listeners window now has Enable and Disable for each analyzer, and Enable all and Disable all, for people who manage analyzers. A change that would interrupt an analyzer sending results or an open run asks for confirmation, and the window then reports which analyzers started listening and which did not, with the reason.
