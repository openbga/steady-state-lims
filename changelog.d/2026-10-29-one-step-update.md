**Update to version N** opens one dialog: confirm you reviewed the changes, confirm your identity with a password or security key, and the app downloads, backs up, approves and installs. A downloaded package is kept while you move between tabs, and **Available updates** opens first with a clear status. Approving a downloaded update from a security-key account now uses the key.
